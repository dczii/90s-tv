# LittlePlay Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 25, 2026

LittlePlay is an Android TV app developed by Danilo Zabala II. This policy
explains how LittlePlay handles information when a parent uses the app.

## Information the app handles

LittlePlay does not require a LittlePlay account and does not send personal
information to the developer.

The app stores the following information locally on the Android TV:

- timer settings, deadlines, and playback positions;
- a salted verifier for the parent PIN (not the PIN itself);
- the parent's selected YouTube videos and playlists; and
- if the parent connects YouTube, Google OAuth tokens.

When a parent connects YouTube, LittlePlay requests read-only access to the
parent's YouTube playlists and subscriptions. The app sends OAuth requests,
YouTube Data API requests, and video playback requests directly to Google and
YouTube. Google and YouTube process those requests under their own privacy
policies. The embedded YouTube player may display ads and may collect device,
network, account, and playback information as described by Google.

LittlePlay also offers an optional phone-link page over the local Wi-Fi
network. Links entered there are sent directly from the phone to the TV and
stored on the TV. The developer does not receive them.

## Collection, sharing, and sale

The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.
LittlePlay contains no developer-operated analytics, advertising SDK, or
remote server. Data sent to Google and YouTube is used to provide the
YouTube connection, catalog, and playback features requested by the user.

## Security and retention

Google OAuth tokens are stored using Android secure storage. Other app
settings are stored in the app's private local storage. Data remains on the
TV until the user removes it, disconnects YouTube where available, clears the
app's data, or uninstalls the app.

## Children

LittlePlay is a parent-configured timer and content-selection tool. The
developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Parents control the connected Google account and the content available in
the app.

## Your choices

A parent can avoid connecting a Google account and use manually entered
YouTube links instead. Clearing LittlePlay's app data or uninstalling the app
removes locally stored settings and credentials. Google account access can
also be revoked from the Google Account permissions page.
