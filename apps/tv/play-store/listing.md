# LittlePlay

## Short description

Parent-selected YouTube on TV with simple watch and break timers.

## Full description

LittlePlay gives families a calmer way to watch YouTube on Android TV.

Parents choose the videos and playlists available in the app, set a watch
window and a break window, and then hand over the remote. When watch time
ends, playback stops and LittlePlay shows a break timer. Starting another
watch window always requires an explicit Continue watching action.

Features:

- Parent-selected YouTube videos and playlists
- Simple watch and break timers
- Optional read-only YouTube connection
- Manual YouTube links without connecting an account
- TV-first, remote-friendly controls
- Settings and credentials stored locally on the TV
- No LittlePlay account and no developer-operated analytics

LittlePlay limits viewing only inside LittlePlay. It cannot block the
official YouTube app, prevent uninstalling the app, or stop someone from
clearing app data or changing the device clock.

YouTube playback requires an internet connection. YouTube and the YouTube
logo are trademarks of Google LLC.
